What it gives you

Five questions owners and site directors bring to us, and the answer you get.

  • “What stops if a drone hits our fuel depot or a workshop?”A site map: the hit point, the fire zone, what stops and for how many days.
  • “How much would we lose?”Property damage and downtime in AED, with the cost of one day of downtime shown separately.
  • “What do we protect first on a limited budget?”Measures ranked by the loss each dirham avoids. Some cost nothing: spread stocks, move fuel, sign a backup supply contract in advance.
  • “How do we keep our people safe?”Where people are on each shift, and how warning, shelter and evacuation work. These measures come first.
  • “How do we keep our clients, and what do we show the insurer?”A backup supply plan and the transfer of part of the orders, contract wording, and an evidence pack for the insurer, the bank and the board.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from a fire safety audit or a Civil Defence inspection?

An audit or an inspection checks compliance with requirements. The readiness check answers the owner’s question: how much the company loses in a strike and which measures cut the loss most per dirham. We use those documents as input.

Do you sell or install counter-drone systems?

No. We are not tied to manufacturers or integrators, so our advice is independent. If you need engineering protection, we help you write the requirements for it.

What site data do you need, and what happens to it?

The site plan, a list of buildings, stocks and equipment, revenue by product and key contracts. Everything is shared under NDA and stays with you. For restricted sites we agree the procedure with your security team.

How much time does it take from our people?

The chief engineer and the heads of production, warehouse, logistics and security: one to two hours of interviews each. Management: three hours for the working session in week four.

Can the check be done without a site visit?

Yes, if the site is closed to visitors: we work from the site plan, photos and a video walk-through. Conclusions on fire spread are then less precise, and we say so in the report.

Does the check help with NCEMA 7000?

Yes, as evidence. The report describes critical activities, the impact of their loss over time and the recovery measures, in the terms of AE/SCNS/NCEMA 7000 and ISO 22301. It does not replace a full business continuity management system.

How is this different from the Resilience audit and improvement programme?

The check covers one scenario for one site in five weeks and ends with a roadmap. The programme builds continuity for the whole company: plans, roles, exercises and metrics. 50% of the price of the check is credited if you move to the programme within six months.
