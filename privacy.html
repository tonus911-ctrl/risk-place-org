Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026. This policy explains how risk-place.org ("we") handles personal data, in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) and other applicable law.

What we collect

  • Contact form: name, work email and the message you send us. We use them only to reply to your enquiry.
  • Readiness quiz: your answers and result are processed anonymously. We do not link them to your identity and do not ask for contact details.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics to understand site usage (pages viewed, approximate location, device type). IP addresses are processed by Google; we do not use analytics to identify individuals.

Legal basis and purpose

We process contact data on the basis of your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. We do not sell personal data, do not use it for automated decision-making, and do not send marketing messages without your consent.

Storage and transfers

Form submissions are delivered to us via a secure notification service and stored only as long as needed to handle your enquiry. Analytics data is processed by Google and may be stored outside the UAE; such transfers rely on safeguards recognised under the PDPL.

Your rights

Under the PDPL you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, write to BCM library (RU)مكتبة استمرارية الأعمال (بالروسية) · info@risk-place.org. We reply within a reasonable period and without charge.

Cookies

The site uses only the cookies required for analytics and for remembering your language choice. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will keep working.

Contact

Data controller: risk-place.org. Email: info@risk-place.org.
