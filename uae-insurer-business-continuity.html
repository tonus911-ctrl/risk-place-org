Business continuity for insurers in the UAE

Most organisations plan for their own outage. An insurer also has to plan for its customers' disaster: the day a city floods, claims multiply while the insurer's own staff and offices may be affected. This page sets out what continuity means for an insurer or takaful operator in the UAE, with a 28-point checklist.

Why an insurer's continuity is different

For a manufacturer, the worst day is its own outage. For an insurer, the worst day is a regional event that hits thousands of policyholders at once. In April 2024 record rainfall flooded parts of the UAE, and motor and property insurers received a wave of claims within days, while many of their own employees were stuck at home. Continuity for an insurer therefore has two halves: keeping its own operations running, and absorbing a surge in demand at the same moment. Insurers in the UAE are supervised by the Central Bank of the UAE, whose operational resilience expectations are a useful frame.

The claims surge scenario

  • Capacity plan for three to five times normal volume in claims intake, surveys and approvals.
  • Surge contracts with a second third-party administrator and temporary staff, agreed before the event.
  • Online and app notification that does not depend on the contact centre.
  • Loss adjuster and surveyor panel that can be extended beyond the usual names.
  • A triage rule that puts vulnerable policyholders and medical cases first.
  • Daily reporting of backlog and time to first contact to management.

Systems and data

The core policy and claims systems need tested recovery within their RTO, and claims must be payable if the core system is down: a manual approval and payment route with limits. At least one backup copy should be immutable or offline. Customer data in recovery sites must respect data protection and sector rules on where data may be stored; the same questions are covered for all sectors on the disaster recovery page.

Partners: TPAs, garages, reinsurers and brokers

An insurer's claims chain runs through partners. Ask third-party administrators and garage networks to show their continuity plans, write reinsurance notification steps into the incident plan, and give brokers and agents a contact route that works during an outage. Evidence that a business-interruption claim will be paid is covered on the insurance evidence page.

Communication

Prepare templates in English and Arabic for policyholders, brokers, the regulator and the media; keep a static status page that works when core systems do not; log complaints during the disruption, because they become the regulator's first question afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Who supervises insurers in the UAE?

The Central Bank of the UAE supervises insurance companies, including their governance and operational resilience. Check the current regulations that apply to your licence.

What is a claims surge scenario?

A planning scenario in which a regional event, such as a flood or a storm, multiplies claims within days while the insurer's own staff and offices may be affected. It tests capacity, partners and communication at once.

Which services of an insurer are critical?

Usually claims notification, medical approvals, motor claims, policy issuance and renewal, claims payment and customer contact. Each needs a tolerance set in a business impact analysis.

How should an insurer test its business continuity?

At least once a year with a claims surge exercise that involves claims, operations, IT and key partners, plus technical recovery tests of the core policy and claims systems.
