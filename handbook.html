Free handbook · EN + AR

BCM without illusions
Gulf edition

How to protect your company's cash flow. A practical map of business continuity for organisations in the GCC — from spotting your weak points to running the business on the day the systems go down. Free, in English and Arabic.

What is inside

Why cash flow is fragileThe executive's blind spot, the real cost of a day of downtime, and the four threats that most often bring a business down.
Assessment: where you will breakSingle points of failure, the three-question stress-test for every critical link.
BIA to ISO 22301Business impact analysis in plain words: RTO, RPO, MTPD — and the mark of a BIA that is actually finished.
IT resilienceTested recovery, the 3-2-1 rule, concentration risk and the Red-Amber-Green response model.
People under pressureWhy teams freeze in a crisis, what stress does to decisions, and how to rehearse before it matters.
Regulators in the GulfISO 22301, NCEMA, SAMA and financial-centre requirements — the pattern behind them and what it means for you.
Working when everything is downA practical scenario: tomorrow your CRM and ERP are down. How to keep earning today.
90-day roadmapSee, strengthen, rehearse: a realistic three-month plan for first results.

The handbook is written for owners and executives, not for continuity specialists. No jargon for its own sake, every part ends with a mini-case from real practice, and it reads in about forty minutes.
