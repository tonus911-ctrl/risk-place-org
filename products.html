SAFE — workplace safety for site crews

A safety programme for the people on the site rather than the people in the office: 32 chapters in five modules, English and Arabic, three industry editions, completed within one shift on any phone. Heat stress, permit discipline, the first hour after an incident, and a named certificate that can be verified. Not to be confused with ERGP Essentials below — Essentials teaches business continuity to office staff, SAFE teaches occupational safety to crews on site.
