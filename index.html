Cyber threat

Ransomware & cyber resilience

Encryption is not an IT incident — it is a CEO exam. The three decisions that decide the outcome are taken before the attack, outside the IT department.

  • —Isolated, restore-tested backups — budgeted by the CFO, not hoped for
  • —First-hours playbook: who shuts the network, who talks to clients
  • —Emergency-mode revenue: selling and serving at 30% capacity
  • —Team roles and first steps rehearsed in advance — no confusion on day X

Average public-case downtime is about a week. What does a week without systems cost you?
