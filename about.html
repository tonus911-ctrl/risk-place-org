WHO WE ARE

About risk-place.org

We are practitioners in business continuity and operational risk, working to ISO 22301, NCEMA guidance and international good practice. Everything we certify and consult on, we also publish — free and open, in Arabic and English.

What we believe

Knowledge should be open. Our library — 215+ pages and the only specialised Arabic-English glossary online — has no paywall and no registration wall. We earn on certification, tools and consulting, not on locking up the basics.

Resilience should be measurable. A continuity programme that cannot show the board a number — the cost of a downtime day, the recovery target against reality — is a folder, not a system. Our dashboard and audit exist to turn folders into numbers.

The Gulf deserves its own voice. The region's regulators — NCEMA, SAMA, the central banks and financial centres — have built serious requirements, yet almost all professional education still speaks only English. ERGP is the first resilience governance certification fully available in Arabic.

The team behind it

The programme is built by practitioners with twenty and more years in risk and continuity functions of large industrial, financial and service organisations, and it is reviewed by an invited circle of senior experts — leaders with 20+ years of practice who examine capstones and keep the bar honest. As the circle grows, we will introduce its members here.
