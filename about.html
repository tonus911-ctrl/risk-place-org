The ERGP Circle

Passing ERGP admits you to the Circle — a private community of practitioners who hold the certificate, coordinated by the two founders. The Circle is small today and grows with every certificate issued.

Membership is earned once, at the examination, and carries three things with it.

KnowledgeAhead of the market
  • A quarterly Circle session: one real case, dissected with the founders and the examiners
  • New modules and regulatory readings — NCEMA, CBUAE, SAMA — reach members before publication
  • Working templates from live engagements
PeopleA directory you can call
  • A private member directory with specialisation and contacts, open to members only
  • Direct access to the examiners and to each other
  • Peers who solve the same problem in the next industry
OpportunitiesCalled upon for real work
  • We assign Circle members to client engagements — audits, dashboards, continuity systems
  • Members with standing become examiners and co-authors of modules
  • Your name in the public register, verifiable by any employer

How you join.

STEP 1Complete the programme — six modules, 94 chapters, six artefacts of your own.
STEP 2Pass six module tests and defend the capstone before the examiner.
STEP 3Receive the certificate — and the invitation to the Circle with it.

How the practice works

We came to resilience from inside organisations.

The plans we write for clients we first executed ourselves — in an industrial giant, in an international bank, under a regulator's inspection. Our plans are short and written for the worst day.

We count risk in money.

A conversation about the cost of a downtime day ends with a decision. In every engagement a model appears where a disruption reaches revenue and profit.

We build the system together with the client's team.

We assemble the recovery scheme in working sessions with those who will act, and leave the organisation a shared language along with the documents.

The practice covers industry, finance, insurance, transport, IT and retail; ISO 22301 and AE/SCNS/NCEMA 7000; preparing organisations for audits and for the conversation with the regulator. More than eight hundred professionals have completed our programmes. The methodology is published openly — in the library and the glossary on this site.

Examiners of the Circle

Capstones are examined by the senior tier of the Circle — invited practitioners with 20+ years in risk and continuity who keep the bar honest. As the Circle grows, its members with standing join them. Senior practitioners who wish to lend the Arabic edition their judgement can apply on the programme page.
