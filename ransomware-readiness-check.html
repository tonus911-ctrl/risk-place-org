Frequently asked questions

Will you hack our systems?

No. The readiness check looks at how the company gets through an attack: processes, backups and management decisions. If you need an outside test of your defences, a specialised information-security partner runs a penetration test and a simulated attack under a separate contract and NDA with you.

Will you stop our ERP for the test?

No. We test the restore on a test bench or in an agreed window. Your IT does everything in the systems; we observe and time it.

We have a security team and backups. Why do we need this?

The security team protects you from the attack. The check answers the owner’s next question: what happens if protection fails. How sales and the warehouse work without systems, how long a restore really takes and what each day of downtime costs.

What data do you need, and who sees it?

A list of processes and systems, your backup routine, and key contracts with customers and suppliers. Everything is shared under NDA, and the report stays with you.

How much time does it take from our people?

IT: one to two days for the restore test. The heads of sales, warehouse, production and finance: one to two hours of interviews each. Management: three hours in the exercise.

Does it help with Central Bank or NCEMA 7000 requirements?

It gives you dated evidence: a restore test with measured times, an exercise with recorded findings and a plan of fixes. Evidence like this feeds both an operational resilience framework and a business continuity system under AE/SCNS/NCEMA 7000. The check does not replace either of them.

How is this different from the Resilience audit and improvement programme?

The check covers one scenario in five weeks and ends with a report to the owner. The programme builds continuity for the whole company: plans, roles, exercises and metrics. 50% of the price of the check is credited if you move to the programme within six months.
