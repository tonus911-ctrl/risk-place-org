Verify a certificate

Every certificate issued by risk-place.org carries a unique number. Enter it below, or scan the QR code printed on the document.

The number is printed in the lower left corner of the certificate.

About this register

The register is kept by risk-place.org and answers in real time. A certificate that has been revoked stays in the register and is shown as revoked — it does not quietly disappear. If a document you were shown does not appear here, it was not issued by us.
