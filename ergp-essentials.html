ERGP Essentials

Business continuity basics for every employee. A lightened, shortened edition of the ERGP flagship programme, rebuilt for people whose job is not risk management — so that the whole organisation, not just the continuity team, knows what to do before and during a disruption.

Why organisations run it

Culture

A healthy, everyday risk culture

Employees stop treating risk as someone else's paperwork. Raising a concern becomes normal, quick and safe — the foundation every continuity system actually stands on.

Prevention

Staff who spot risks before they fire

The course trains preventive identification: seeing single points of failure at your own desk, naming them and reporting them — so problems surface as observations, not incidents.

Readiness

Faster, calmer action in a crisis

Every participant learns the first-hour drill: their role, whom to alert, what to do when systems are down, and what never to do. Speed comes from rehearsal, not from bravery.

Proof

Evidence for auditors and regulators

A named certificate for each employee and a completion report for HR — documented awareness training that continuity audits and regulators increasingly expect to see.

The programme — 12 chapters, about two hours

Written in plain language, no jargon walls. Self-paced online: most people finish in two or three sittings. Both editions — English and Arabic — are included in every licence.

  1. What business continuity is — in the words of your own job
  2. What a day of downtime costs, and why prevention is cheaper
  3. The four threats that stop companies most often
  4. My role: what the organisation expects from me before and during a disruption
  5. Single points of failure at my own desk — and how to spot them
  6. How to raise a risk: quick, specific, blame-free
  7. The first 60 minutes of an incident: the all-staff drill
  8. Who speaks and who does not: communication during an incident
  9. Red — Amber — Green: how the response is staged
  10. Working when the systems are down: the paper-and-phone mode
  11. After the incident: honest lessons instead of blame
  12. Final test and your named certificate

How it works

  • Online and self-paced — each employee studies from any device, progress is saved.
  • About two hours in total — designed for busy people, in short chapters with one idea each.
  • Checkpoint questions and a final test — passing it issues a named certificate; HR receives a completion report for the whole group.
  • English and Arabic editions — every employee studies in the language they think in; both are included.
  • Built from ERGP — the same authors and methodology as the executive programme, compressed for a general audience.

Frequently asked questions

Is the course available now? We are taking early corporate bookings; first groups start in autumn 2026. Early companies get a sample chapter on request, priority onboarding and the launch price locked.
